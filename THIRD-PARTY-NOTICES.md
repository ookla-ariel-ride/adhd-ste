## ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English

ASD-STE100 (Issue 9) is a standard published by ASD, the AeroSpace, Security and Defence Industries Association of Europe, which holds its copyright. `ADHD-STE.md` paraphrases a small number of its writing rules, through the gist above. It does not reproduce the standard or its dictionary. Output that follows `ADHD-STE.md` is "STE style", not certified STE, and this project has no affiliation with ASD.
